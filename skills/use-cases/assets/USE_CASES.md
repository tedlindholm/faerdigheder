Move a queued row or proposal here when work starts. Use the outcome as a heading. Keep the draft, links to any sketch under Plans, and a short note of what remains. A case stays here while implementation, tests, writing or a required sketch are incomplete. When all are ready and the tests pass, follow the finished folder’s README to move and verify the case.
